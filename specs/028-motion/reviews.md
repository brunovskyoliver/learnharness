## Open for the owner

- **Library LCP on the throttled profile, +768ms.** ~510ms is Chrome stamping LCP when the late list's compositor ink-in (280ms) ends under 4× CPU: with animations off the empty line's LCP is 18ms after its data (`lcp-after-noanim.json`), so the line is visible about when it was before. ~300ms is real: the Library's data request starts ~300ms later (5,115 → 5,416ms) and first paint is 124ms later (1,860 → 1,984ms), consistently. The startup JS is +2.4% gzip (+6.5KB) and the CSS +3.0KB gzip; neither explains all of it, and no long task grew. Startup stays inside the +5% budget (SC-006); research R5's "hydration is not delayed" does not hold under this profile. Options: accept; or profile hydration on the throttled profile as a follow-up.
- **The soak's first frame is the browser's.** In this headless, GPU-less browser the first ~100ms of any root view transition show the old frame (more on a page running WebGL), so the soak's first frames play unseen and it appears ~40-60% grown. A GPU (the owner's Electron) should be much faster, but that is not measured here. If it still feels late, the remaining lever is starting the transition on pointerdown instead of click (hides ~80-120ms of a human click, but commits the theme before the click is complete, and a drag-off would need cancelling). Not done: it changes the control's behaviour.
- **Saving a theme that fails.** The theme shows at once and the PUT runs alongside it (it was optimistic already). On failure Customize keeps the change shown and shows its failed line with Retry (feature 025's rule). Reverting the soak on failure would change that rule; not done.
- **Right pane open (not 028).** Opening the right pane stalls ~130ms before its first frame, and the lesson column re-centres at once while the pane slides in (`recordings/*-r12-right-pane-a`). It is layout and rendering, not a catalogue motion; a smoother column move would be new motion.
